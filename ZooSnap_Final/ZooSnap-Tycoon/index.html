ZooSnap Tycoon

How to play

  1. Goal: welcome 3,500 visitors in 8 days.
  2. Adopt animals by holding an animal photo on your phone up to the webcam. Our AI model recognizes the species. Different animals have different costs, appeal and traits.
  3. Upgrade enclosures: click one in the 3D zoo to expand its fence (more animals of that species) or add comfort (happier animals, more visitors).
  4. Watch capacity: the zoo only holds so many visitors at once. Buy a Bigger entrance when the line at the gate gets long.
  5. Pay upkeep every night. Two nights in debt and the bank closes your zoo.
  6. Watch for events. When an animal escapes or a visitor asks to see one, show the right photo fast. Other events give you a choice.
  7. Your ending depends on visitors, reputation and the animals you collect.
